What you'll learn

Every program is built around practice time on the clinic floor, not just the textbook.

Barber training program

Cutting, fading, tapering, razor work and beard design, plus the sanitation, safety and state-law knowledge you need to pass your licensing exams.

  • Clippers, shears and razor fundamentals
  • Fades, line-ups and classic cuts
  • Shaves, beards and hot towel service
  • Exam prep and shop management basics

Student clinic

Walk in for a haircut or shave from a student, supervised by a licensed instructor, at student prices.
